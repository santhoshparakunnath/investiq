# Purpose

This document describes the architectural principles behind InvestIQ.

The architecture is designed around one central idea:

> **Transform historical investment data into investment intelligence.**

Every component in the system exists to support this objective.

---

# Architectural Philosophy

InvestIQ is not built as a traditional portfolio management application.

Instead, it is built as a layered Intelligence Platform.

Each layer has a single responsibility.

```
Raw Data

↓

Analytics

↓

Behaviour

↓

Evidence

↓

Insights

↓

AI Coach
```

This separation ensures that calculations remain deterministic while AI remains explainable.

# Layer 1 — Import Engine

Responsibility

Read data from external sources.

Current Support

- ICICI Direct Tradebook
- ICICI Direct Holdings

Future Support

- Zerodha
- Groww
- Upstox
- Mutual Funds
- ETFs
- Corporate Actions

The Import Engine performs:

- File detection
- Validation
- Mapping
- Normalisation

The Import Engine does not perform analytics.

---

# Layer 2 — Domain Models

Domain models represent business entities.

Examples

Core Models

- Transaction
- Holding
- TradeLot
- Charges

Portfolio Models

- PortfolioSummary
- InvestmentDNA

Future Models

- CompletedInvestment
- BehaviourProfile
- RiskProfile
- Insight

Models should contain data only.

Business logic belongs in services.

---

# Layer 3 — Intelligence Engine

The Intelligence Engine is the heart of InvestIQ.

It transforms historical investment data into measurable knowledge.

The Intelligence Engine is the primary intellectual property of InvestIQ.

It consists of several specialised engines.

---

## Portfolio Intelligence

Answers

> What do I own?

Examples

- Portfolio Value
- Allocation
- Concentration
- Sector Exposure

---

## Investment DNA

Answers

> Who am I as an investor?

Examples

- Years Investing
- Most Traded Stock
- Average Position Size
- Trading Frequency

---

## Behaviour Intelligence

Answers

> How do I invest?

Examples

Buying Behaviour

Selling Behaviour

Holding Behaviour

Risk Behaviour

---

## Performance Intelligence

Answers

> Which decisions created wealth?

Examples

- Win Rate
- Average Winner
- Average Loser
- CAGR
- XIRR

---

# Layer 4 — Evidence Engine

Facts alone are not enough.

Every observation must be supported by evidence.

Example

Observation

"You consistently outperform when holding investments longer than three years."

Evidence

- 42 completed investments
- Average return 31%
- Confidence 93%

The Evidence Engine provides confidence and traceability.

---

# Layer 5 — Insight Engine

The Insight Engine transforms evidence into observations.

Examples

- Your best investments share common characteristics.
- You frequently average down successfully.
- Your strongest investments are concentrated in fewer sectors.

Insights are generated from deterministic analytics.

---

# Layer 6 — AI Coach

The AI Coach is the conversational interface.

Its responsibilities include:

- Explaining insights
- Answering investor questions
- Summarising behaviour
- Providing coaching

The AI Coach does not calculate investment metrics.

It explains results generated by the Intelligence Engine.

---

# Intelligence Before AI

This is one of the fundamental architectural principles of InvestIQ.

```
Transactions

↓

Analytics

↓

Behaviour

↓

Evidence

↓

AI Explanation
```

Never

```
Transactions

↓

AI

↓

Analytics
```

Deterministic analytics ensure that every result is:

- Repeatable
- Testable
- Explainable

# Design Principles

## Single Responsibility

Each service performs one responsibility.

---

## Deterministic Analytics

Business calculations should always produce the same result for the same data.

---

## Testability

Every calculation should have unit tests.

---

## Simplicity

Prefer simple, readable implementations over clever solutions.

---

## Reusability

Shared calculations should be implemented once and reused throughout the platform.

---

Intelligence Engine
