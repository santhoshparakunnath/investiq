# Our Mission

Help investors become better decision-makers by learning from every investment they have ever made.

InvestIQ is built on a simple belief:

> Every investor already possesses years of valuable investing experience.
>
> Unfortunately, that experience is rarely analysed, measured or transformed into knowledge.

Our mission is to convert years of investment history into actionable intelligence that helps investors make better decisions throughout their investing journey.

---

# The Problem

Most investment platforms focus on portfolios.

They answer questions such as:

- What do I own?
- What is my portfolio worth?
- How much did I gain today?
- Which stocks should I buy?

These are useful questions.

However, they ignore a much more important one.

> **What kind of investor am I?**

Every investment represents a decision.

Over time, thousands of decisions reveal patterns, habits, strengths and weaknesses.

Today, those patterns remain hidden.

InvestIQ exists to uncover them.

---

# Our Vision

We believe the future of investing is not simply predicting markets.

It is helping investors understand themselves.

Our long-term vision is to become the world's most trusted Investment Intelligence Platform by helping investors answer questions such as:

- Why have my best investments succeeded?
- Which mistakes do I repeatedly make?
- Which habits consistently create wealth?
- How has my investing behaviour evolved?
- What evidence from my own history should influence my next decision?

# What InvestIQ Is

InvestIQ is an Investment Intelligence Platform.

It transforms historical investment data into knowledge.

The platform combines deterministic analytics, behavioural analysis and artificial intelligence to help investors understand their own decision-making process.

Rather than replacing investors, InvestIQ helps them become better investors.

---

# What InvestIQ Is Not

InvestIQ is not:

- A stock prediction platform.
- A trading platform.
- A portfolio tracker.
- A stock screener.
- A platform that generates speculative buy and sell recommendations.

Many excellent products already exist in those categories.

InvestIQ focuses on a different problem.

Understanding investor behaviour.

---

# Our Philosophy

Markets cannot be controlled.

Behaviour can.

While nobody can consistently predict future markets, every investor can improve the quality of their decisions.

Small improvements in decision-making, repeated over decades, can have a significant impact on long-term wealth creation.

InvestIQ exists to support that journey.

# Core Principles

## Investors before Markets

We study investor behaviour before studying market behaviour.

The goal is not simply to understand investments.

The goal is to understand investors.

---

## Analytics before AI

Artificial Intelligence should explain.

It should not calculate.

Every metric, score and behavioural observation should first be produced by deterministic analytics.

AI then transforms those findings into natural language.

---

## Evidence before Advice

Every recommendation must be supported by measurable evidence.

InvestIQ should never present unsupported opinions.

Every insight should answer:

- What was observed?
- How often did it occur?
- How confident are we?
- What evidence supports this conclusion?

---

## Questions before Features

Every feature begins with an investor question.

Examples include:

- How long do I usually hold investments?
- Do I consistently sell winners too early?
- Which sectors have created the most wealth?
- How has my investing behaviour changed over time?

If a feature does not answer a meaningful investor question, it should not be built.

---

## Ship before Perfection

We value progress over perfection.

InvestIQ is built through small, complete improvements.

Each feature should be:

- Designed
- Implemented
- Tested
- Documented
- Released

before the next feature begins.

---

# Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence is an important part of InvestIQ.

However, AI is not the product.

The Intelligence Engine is the product.

The Intelligence Engine:

- Measures
- Calculates
- Detects patterns
- Produces evidence

Artificial Intelligence:

- Explains
- Summarises
- Coaches
- Answers questions
- Makes the platform conversational

This separation ensures that recommendations remain reproducible, explainable and testable.
